Notesy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Notesy is a handwriting notes app for iPad, made by an independent developer. This policy is short because there is very little to say:

What the developer collects

Nothing. Notesy has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server run by the developer. The developer never sees your notes.

Where your notes live

Your notebooks, drawings, templates, and preferences are stored on your iPad, in the app’s private storage.

iCloud sync (optional). When sync is on, your notebooks and templates also sync through your own private iCloud database, operated by Apple, so they appear on your other iPads signed in to the same Apple Account. The developer has no access to that database. You choose local-only or iCloud when you first open Notesy, and you can turn sync off in Settings. Turning it off stops syncing but does not delete the copies already in iCloud. Notebooks you delete while sync is on are deleted from iCloud too.

If you use Apple’s device backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), your notes are included in those backups under Apple’s terms, like any other app’s data.

Network use

Notesy talks only to Apple: to iCloud when sync is on, and to the App Store when you look at or buy a tip or pledge.

Sharing and exports

When you export a page or notebook (PNG/PDF), the file is handed to Apple’s share sheet and goes only where you send it.

Tips and pledges

The optional tips and monthly or yearly pledges are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Notesy never sees your payment details. The app checks your purchase status with Apple, on your iPad, to turn on the Ember supporter theme for active pledges. Apple handles renewals and cancellations, and Apple’s privacy policy applies to the transaction: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this same address. Questions: open an issue.
