# Notesy Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 7, 2026

Notesy is a handwriting notes app for iPad, made by an independent developer.
This policy is short because there is very little to say:

## What Notesy collects

**Nothing.** Notesy has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no
tracking, and makes no network requests of its own.

## Where your notes live

Your notebooks, drawings, and templates are stored **only on your device**,
in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded anywhere by Notesy.
If you delete the app, its data is deleted with it.

If you use Apple's device backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), your
notes are included in those backups under Apple's terms, like any other app's
data.

## Sharing and exports

When you export a page or notebook (PNG/PDF), the file is handed to Apple's
share sheet and goes only where you send it.

## Donations

The optional "Support development" purchases are processed entirely by Apple
through the App Store. Notesy never sees your payment details. Apple's privacy
policy applies to the transaction: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this same
address. Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/alyakan/notesy-support/issues
