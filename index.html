Notesy

Calm note taking — a warm, minimal handwriting app for iPad.

Getting help

Found a bug or have a feature idea? Open an issue — it goes straight to the developer.

Questions about tips, pledges or purchases? Tips and the monthly or yearly pledges are optional and handled entirely by Apple. Tips unlock nothing; an active pledge turns on the Ember supporter theme. To restore a pledge on a new iPad, tap Restore purchases in Support development. Manage or cancel a pledge in your App Store account settings, and refunds go through Apple’s Report a Problem page.

Tips

  • Tools live behind the small tab on the edge of the paper — pull it out.
  • Pause mid-stroke to snap it into a straight line (can be turned off in Settings).
  • Turn pages by swiping with two fingers (one finger when “Draw with finger” is off).
  • Build your own paper in Settings → Page templates: place checkboxes, boxes, and lines, or draw your own — then pick it for any notebook.

Privacy

Notesy collects no data. Optional iCloud sync uses your own private iCloud database, which the developer cannot see. Read the privacy policy.
